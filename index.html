About

I am Rubem, a photographer and designer drawn to honest moments, expressive movement, and distinctive visual details. I create images across street life, travel, portraits, sport, and creative spaces, helping people and places tell a stronger visual story. My approach blends thoughtful composition with experimentation, from natural light to slow shutter and flash techniques, so every frame feels personal, atmospheric, and alive.

Based in New Zealand, across Auckland, Tauranga and Raglan, and available to travel when the trip is covered. Every booking starts with a quick chat, so we agree on the look before the day.
